# Design brief: lower hand-arm vibration on a hand-held powered tool

A 9 kg hand-held powered tool (two handles, rotating drive, used outdoors on building sites) exposes the operator
to a vibration total value of about 7.5 m/s² at the rear handle. Operators may then only use it for a short part
of a working day before reaching the daily exposure limit. The next product generation should let them work longer.

## Must requirements
- Vibration total value at the rear handle at most 4.0 m/s² in the standard test.
- Added mass at most 0.6 kg compared with today's tool.
- Works from -15 °C to +40 °C.
- Survives 500 h in dust and rain (IP54 level), with no exposed moving parts that jam.

## Should requirements
- Unit cost increase at most 15 %.
- No change to the drive train if it can be avoided.
- Handle feel and control stay at least as good as today.
