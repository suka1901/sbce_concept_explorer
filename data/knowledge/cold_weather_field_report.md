# Field report: handle isolators in Nordic winter

## Observation
On the previous generation, natural-rubber handle mounts stiffened at about -15 °C. Measured handle
vibration rose by about 25 percent compared with summer, and some mounts cracked after one season.

## Fix
A silicone-based compound kept its stiffness down to -30 °C and removed the cracking. It cost about
8 percent more per mount set than natural rubber.
