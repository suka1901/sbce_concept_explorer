# Elastomer handle isolators

## Principle and typical effect
Rubber mounts between the handle and the housing act as a low-pass filter. With the mount natural frequency
well below the dominant excitation frequency, handle vibration typically drops by 30 to 50 percent.
Softer mounts isolate better but make the handle feel loose and reduce control.

## Mass, cost and packaging
Four natural-rubber mounts add about 0.1 kg. Unit cost increase is small, roughly 2 to 4 percent.
They fit in the existing handle interface with minor tooling changes.

## Temperature behaviour
Natural rubber stiffens below about -10 °C. Stiffer mounts raise the natural frequency and the isolation
effect falls. See the cold-weather field report for what happened on the previous generation.
