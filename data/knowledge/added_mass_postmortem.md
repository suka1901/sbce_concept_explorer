# Post-mortem: added ballast mass in the housing (dropped concept)

## What was tried
A 1.2 kg steel ballast was added inside the housing to lower the vibration amplitude.

## Result
Handle vibration fell by only about 20 percent. Operators complained that the heavier tool was more tiring
to hold and position, and the mass target was exceeded by a wide margin. The concept was dropped.

## Lesson
Extra mass is a weak lever for handle vibration and conflicts directly with the mass requirement.
