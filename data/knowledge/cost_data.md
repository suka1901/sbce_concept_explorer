# Indicative cost data for handle vibration solutions

## Unit cost increase (approximate)
- Natural-rubber mounts: 2 to 4 percent
- Silicone-compound mounts: about 3 to 5 percent
- Steel springs with guides and boots: 6 to 8 percent
- Tuned mass absorber: about 5 percent
- Parallel linkage with sealed bushings and damper: about 12 percent
- Active cancellation with battery: about 35 percent

## Tooling
New handle castings cost about the same for all passive concepts; the linkage needs the most new tooling.
