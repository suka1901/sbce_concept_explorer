# Steel spring handle suspension

## Principle and typical effect
Coil springs with guide pins carry the handle. Springs keep their stiffness over the whole temperature range.
Bench tests on a similar tool gave 40 to 60 percent lower handle vibration.

## Design issues
Springs have almost no damping, so end stops and a small damper are needed to avoid resonance at start-up.
Guide pins wear if dust enters the guides; a rubber boot over each guide solved this in a later design.
Added mass about 0.25 kg. Unit cost increase about 6 to 8 percent.
