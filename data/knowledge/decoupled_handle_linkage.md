# Decoupled handle on a parallel linkage

## Principle and typical effect
The handle is carried on a parallel link mechanism with a spring and a damper, so it can move along the
main vibration direction while staying parallel. Prototype tests gave 60 to 75 percent lower vibration.

## Design issues
More parts: added mass about 0.5 kg and unit cost about plus 12 percent. The pivots wear quickly in dust
unless sealed bushings are used. With sealed bushings the linkage passed a 500 h dust test.
