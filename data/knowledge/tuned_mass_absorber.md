# Tuned mass absorber on the handle

## Principle and typical effect
A small spring-mass system tuned to the dominant excitation frequency cancels vibration at that frequency.
At the tuned frequency, reductions of 50 to 70 percent were measured on a test rig.

## Limitations
The effect is narrow-band. On tools with variable speed, or when the operating speed drifts with load,
the reduction falls to 10 to 20 percent away from the tuned frequency. Absorber mass is usually 0.3 to 0.8 kg.
Cost increase about 5 percent.
