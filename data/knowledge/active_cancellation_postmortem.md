# Post-mortem: active vibration cancellation handle (stopped project)

## What was tried
An electromagnetic actuator in the handle, driven by an accelerometer and a controller, produced a
counter-force. In the lab it reduced handle vibration by about 70 percent.

## Why it was stopped
The actuator and controller drew around 40 W and needed a battery pack, which added 0.9 kg.
Unit cost rose by about 35 percent. Two of three prototypes failed the dust test because the actuator
air gap filled with fine dust. The project was stopped after the field test.

## Lesson
Do not restart active cancellation for site tools unless power, mass and sealing are solved first.
